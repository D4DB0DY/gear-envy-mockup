Gear envy is a solved problem.

01 / The insight

Great gear spends most of its life doing nothing.

A cinema camera earns its keep on set, then waits. A drone flies one weekend in four. Premium kit is some of the most underused equipment in the country — and some of the most wanted.

Gear Envy activates it. Owners put idle kit to work. Renters shoot with gear they'd never justify buying. Everyone gets more weekends out of the same equipment.

02 / For renters

Stop wanting it. Start shooting with it.

  1. Step 01

    Find the kit for the job

    Browse cameras and drones from verified Sydney owners. Pick your dates, see the real daily price upfront — no hidden extras.

  2. Step 02

    Book and pick up locally

    Reserve in a couple of taps, then collect from the owner at a time that suits. Every handover includes a condition check, so both sides know exactly what's what.

  3. Step 03

    Shoot, then hand it back

    Do the work. Return the gear in the shape you got it. Try something different next weekend — that's the whole point.
