01 / The maths

Idle gear is a depreciating asset. Rented gear isn't.

Camera bodies lose value on the shelf whether you use them or not. Renting yours out a couple of weekends a month changes the equation — the gear pays down its own cost while you're not using it.

03 / What's expected

The lender side of the deal.

Renters trust the platform because lenders keep a few simple commitments. Here's the standard we hold the catalogue to.

  1. Expectation 01

    Honest listings

    Accurate photos, real condition notes, every scratch disclosed. Surprises kill trust — for you and for the platform.

  2. Expectation 02

    Gear that works

    Everything listed must be fully functional, charged, cleaned and packed with the essentials — batteries, cards, chargers, straps.

  3. Expectation 03

    Responsive handovers

    Reply to requests within [PENDING — RESPONSE SLA], honour approved bookings, and show up on time at both ends of the rental.
